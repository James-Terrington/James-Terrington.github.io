HERMES

Group design project · Team of nine

Aerodynamics lead on a long-endurance autonomous aircraft for scientific exploration of Titan. I sized the 10.14 m² high-aspect-ratio wing inside the entry capsule's stowage constraints, reaching a minimum cruise L/D of 17.3, and led the mission cost breakdown.

2026

Nox

Master's dissertation · Individual

Rebuilt a conditional GAN that removes foreground stars from astrophotography so the nebula behind them can be studied. New synthetic training data and a ResNet50V2 encoder cleared the halo artefacts the original left around bright stars, finishing 5.6 dB of PSNR above the baseline.

  • Python
  • TensorFlow
  • Computer vision
2025

CFD projects

Advanced CFD · Individual

Two studies. A drag prediction of the DLR-F6 wing–body–pylon–nacelle benchmarked against AIAA Drag Prediction Workshop wind tunnel data, landing within 3% at the Ma 0.75 cruise datum; and a conjugate heat transfer study of combustor effusion cooling, where an adiabatic comparison isolated a 110% higher peak cooling effectiveness from conduction.

  • STAR-CCM+
  • Mesh generation
  • Turbulence modelling
2025

Military jet trainer

Aircraft Design · Team of five

Preliminary design of an advanced trainer to replace the RAF Hawk T2. I covered constraint analysis, selection of the design point, the mission profile, sizing to MTOW, and the retractable tricycle undercarriage CAD. Eight of ten brief requirements met.

  • Constraint analysis
  • Mission sizing
  • Siemens NX
2024

Gas turbine engine

Gas Turbine Design · Team of two

A high-bypass turbofan for a large civil airliner, sized to 16,000 lbf cruise thrust at Mach 0.85. Cycle selected in GasTurb, then annulus sizing and free-vortex velocity triangles from the fan through to the LPT, with HPT cooling flow traded against SFC across a 6–12% sweep. Thirty-two of thirty-four design criteria met.

  • GasTurb
  • Turbomachinery
  • Siemens NX
  • BS 8888
2023

Wing structure

Structural design project · Team of three

A load-bearing wing structure taken from concept to toleranced CAD of the laser-cut parts, then to the assembly drawing and written work instructions a group of first years built it from without us present. Best structural rigidity in the cohort.

  • Siemens NX
  • Technical drawing
  • Laser cutting
2022

Control-line aircraft

Design and build · Team of five

A balsa and ply control-line aircraft taken from NX assembly through workshop manufacture to flight test in a single week. Successful take-off, circuit and landing in testing; the airframe was lost to a nose-down impact on the assessed flight.

  • Workshop manufacture
  • Siemens NX
  • Flight testing
